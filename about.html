Origin Story

The X&Y Journey

Founded by Vasudha Goenka, X&Y began with a single question: Why shouldn't Kolkata have a bistro that takes artisanal sourdough and slow culinary craft this seriously?

2023

THE TRAINING – The Foundation

Vasudha graduates in nutrition and culinary arts, coming home to Kolkata with a vision. She looked for a place that held those same standards and decided to build it herself.

"If it doesn't exist here, maybe that's the point."
FEB 2024

ALIPORE – The First Door

X&Y opens in Alipore. Sourdough starter ferments for 18 hours, croissants are laminated by hand layer by layer. Within weeks, guests arrive before the doors unlock.

FEB 2025

SALT LAKE – Sector 2

Expanding to Salt Lake without altering the core menu or quality. Bringing the same hollandaise and belief in doing a small number of things properly.

JULY 2025

HUNGERFORD STREET – Elgin Road

A central neighbourhood space close to schools and offices—built for easy, unplanned visits, decompressing after class, or lingering over a second cup of coffee.

Our Pillars

Three Things We Stand For

The guiding values behind every loaf of bread, dish, and table at X&Y.

We bake like no one's watching the clock

Our sourdough ferments for 18 hours. Our croissants are laminated by hand, layer by layer. We could cut corners — we've just never wanted to.

"The difference between good and extraordinary is always time."

Built for Kolkata, by someone who couldn't stop thinking about it

Vasudha came back to this city with one question she couldn't shake — why isn't there a place here that takes food this seriously? X&Y is the answer.

"This city deserved it. So we built it."

Your table is yours for as long as you need it

We don't hover. We don't rush. If you came in for breakfast and you're still here at lunch — perfect. Some of the best things happen slowly.

"The best café is the one you never want to leave."

X&Y Bakehouse & Bistro. Crafted with patience, passion, and fresh Kolkata spirit.
